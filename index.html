How we plan to protect you

Sending money should never feel risky. We plan to work with licensed partners, verify identity as the law requires, and ask you to confirm every transfer.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Zuvana available now?

Not yet. Zuvana is coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll text or email you when we open.

How much will it cost?

We plan to charge a flat $1.99 fee per transfer at launch, and we're aiming for the real exchange rate. You'll see the exact rate and total before you confirm.

How will my family receive the money?

We plan to deliver to bank accounts in Mexico through licensed partners. Your family gets a link to choose their account.

Which countries will you support?

We're starting with the United States to Mexico. More countries are planned after launch.
