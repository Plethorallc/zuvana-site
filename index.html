FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I send money with Zuvana today?

Not yet. Zuvana is coming soon and this site is a waitlist only — no money can be sent today. Join the list and we'll let you know when we open.

How much will it cost?

We're planning a flat $1.99 fee per transfer at launch. Pricing isn't final and may change; you'll always see the fee before you confirm.

What exchange rate will I get?

We're aiming for the real exchange rate with no hidden markup. The final rate will be shown before you confirm each transfer.

How will my family receive the money?

They'll get a link by text and receive the money in their bank account (cash pickup options planned).

Which chat apps will work?

We're planning to support WhatsApp, iMessage and regular SMS, so there's no new app to download.

Where will Zuvana be available?

We plan to start with sending from the U.S. to Mexico. More countries may come later.

What do you do with my phone number or email?

We only use it to contact you about launch. We don't sell it. Read our Privacy Policy.
