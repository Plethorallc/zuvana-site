FAQ

Questions about how it works

Do I need to download an app?

We're designing Zuvana to work inside chat apps you already have. Details will be shared before launch.

What if I make a mistake in my message?

Nothing is sent until you review the details and confirm. If something looks wrong, just don't confirm and start again.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery times will depend on our partners and the receiving bank. We'll share expected times before launch and show them before you confirm.

Can my family reply in Spanish?

Yes. Every message and screen is planned in Spanish and English.
