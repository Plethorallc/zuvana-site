How we plan to protect you

Trust has to be earned. Here's how we plan to look after you and your money when Zuvana launches, and how to protect yourself from scams today.

Zuvana is not yet available. This page describes our plans, which may change and will depend on regulatory approvals and partner agreements.

Illustrative photo

Our approach

What we plan to do

Licensed partners

We plan to work with licensed partners to move and deliver money.

Identity checks

We will verify identity as the law requires, to help keep accounts in the right hands.

You confirm every transfer

Nothing is sent until you review the details and reply to confirm.

Careful with your data

We plan to collect only what we need and explain how we use it in our Privacy Policy.

Our promise

Zuvana will never…

If someone claiming to be Zuvana does any of these things, it isn't us. Stop and email hello@zuvana.app.

  • Ask for your PIN, password or one-time codes by text, email or phone
  • Send money without your confirmation
  • Ask you to pay with gift cards
  • Pressure you to send money “right now” to fix a problem
  • Charge you to join the waitlist

Scam awareness

Protect yourself from money transfer scams

These tips apply to any money transfer service, not just Zuvana.

Only send to people you know

Never send money to someone you've only met online or by phone.

Slow down when rushed

Urgency is a classic scam sign: “your relative is in trouble, pay now”. Call your family directly first.

Prizes don't cost money

If you have to pay a fee to receive a prize, a loan or a job, it's likely a scam.

Keep codes private

Never share one-time codes or passwords, even with someone who says they work for a company.

Check the details

Before confirming any transfer, check the name, amount and account carefully.

Use official links

Our only website is zuvana.app. Be careful with look-alike addresses.

Report a concern

Seen something suspicious?

If you got a message, call or website claiming to be Zuvana that doesn't look right, tell us. If you've lost money to a scam, also contact your bank and local authorities.

Email our team

In English or Spanish. Please include a screenshot if you can. Never send us passwords or full card numbers.
