Getting started

What is Zuvana?

Zuvana is a planned service from BlockPay LLC for sending money from the United States to Mexico by text message. It isn't available yet.

When will Zuvana launch?

We don't have a launch date to share yet. Launch depends on regulatory approvals and partner agreements. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you when we open.

Which countries will you support?

We're starting with transfers from the United States to Mexico. More countries are planned after launch, and we'll announce them to the waitlist first.

Who is behind Zuvana?

Zuvana is a project of BlockPay LLC, a California company. You can reach us at hello@zuvana.app.

Sending money

How will sending by text work?

You'll write something like “send $50 to Mom” in a chat. We'll show you the full price and ask you to confirm. Nothing is sent until you reply yes.

Which chat apps will work?

We're planning for WhatsApp, iMessage and SMS. Availability on each will depend on those platforms and may change.

What will I need to sign up?

We expect to ask for a U.S. phone number, a valid ID and a way to pay. We will verify identity as the law requires. Final requirements will be shared before launch.

Will there be sending limits?

Probably, as with most money transfer services. Limits will depend on the law and our partners, and we'll publish them before launch.

Receiving money

How will my family receive the money?

We plan to deliver to bank accounts in Mexico through licensed partners. Your family member gets a message with a link to choose their account.

Will my family need an app?

We're designing it so they don't: they'll receive a link by message. Details will be confirmed before launch.

How long will it take to arrive?

Delivery times will depend on our partners and the receiving bank. We'll show the expected time before you confirm.

Fees and exchange rates

How much will a transfer cost?

We plan to charge a flat $1.99 fee per transfer at launch. Fees may change before launch and will depend on regulatory approvals and partner agreements.

What exchange rate will you use?

We're aiming for the real exchange rate. You'll see the exact rate and how many pesos your family receives before you confirm.

Are there hidden fees?

Our plan is to show every cost on one screen before you confirm, with no percentage fees added on top.

Safety and privacy

How will you protect my money?

We plan to work with licensed partners to move money, and to ask you to confirm every transfer. Read our safety page for details.

Will Zuvana ever ask for my PIN or password?

No. We'll never ask for your PIN, password or one-time codes by text, email or phone. If someone does, it isn't us.

What do you do with my waitlist details?

We use them only to contact you about launch. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Waitlist and account

How do I join the waitlist?

Enter your phone number or email in any waitlist form on this site. It's free.

How do I stop launch texts or leave the list?

Reply STOP to any text, or email hello@zuvana.app and we'll remove you.

Do I get anything for joining early?

Waitlist members will be the first to hear when Zuvana opens.
